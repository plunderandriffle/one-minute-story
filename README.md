# One Minute Story

Build a mobile first landing page called “One Minute.” Center a beveled phone mockup that cycles through short founder and creator segments. Add timed captions, a subtle progress bar, and smooth transitions. Use the headline: “YOUR STORY. ONE MINUTE.” Primary CTA: “MAKE MY FIRST MINUTE.” Secondary CTA: “WATCH THE DEMO.” Below the fold, show four examples: Founder Minutes, Creator Minutes, Business Minutes, and Personal Minutes. Clicking any loads a sample segment into the phone. For creation, offer three options. Upload a voice note, type an idea, or enter a business URL. Keep it simple, premium, minimal text, smooth transitions, and optimized for mobile. No walls of copy. Let the phone do the selling.
